# Somthingreat catalogue-gap report

This report records known catalogue limits after introducing mastery-aware
foundation and Skills policies. It is a development reference; no exercises
listed below were added as part of the policy refactor.

## Pull foundation

- Without a pull-up bar, there are no enabled primary horizontal-pull
  identities. Household-anchor row exercises remain disabled because the app
  cannot verify the table, chair, or towel anchor.
- With a pull-up bar, a beginner has Active hang preparation and Scapular
  pull-up plus the Prone pull-down accessory. A four-exercise Pull foundation
  workout therefore has very little rotation capacity.
- Pull-up Skills must reuse some of those identities at beginner level. The
  generator records controlled overlap and changes the repeated dose where
  supported.

Recommended future catalogue work:

- Add at least two beginner-safe, no-anchor Pull foundation identities.
- Add another bar-based grip or scapular-control preparation that is distinct
  from Active hang preparation and Scapular pull-up.
- Do not restore unverified household-anchor rows.

## Beginner Handstand

- The first unlocked distinct preparation identities are Wrist preparation and
  Pike hold. Several track entries reference the same stable IDs.
- Anti-extension body-line work is therefore used as explicit skill
  preparation to complete a beginner Skills session.
- Foundation Push and Handstand Skills may both require Wrist preparation until
  more eligible preparation identities unlock.

Recommended future catalogue work:

- Add a beginner shoulder-elevation drill with a distinct stable identity.
- Add a low-risk wall body-line drill before the current wall-hold progression.
- Add a beginner serratus-control exercise that does not duplicate Pike hold.

## Beginner L-sit

- The first distinct identities are Seated compression lift and Reverse crunch.
- Beginner support-strength preparation is currently supplied from the enabled
  dip-strength catalogue.
- Direct L-sit progression has limited same-day rotation before later support
  and tuck stages unlock.

Recommended future catalogue work:

- Add a beginner seated leg-lift preparation distinct from Seated compression
  lift.
- Add an equipment-free straight-arm support preparation.
- Add a hamstring-mobility identity that can be explicitly classified as L-sit
  preparation and Lower Body mobility.

## General fitness introductory Skills

- General fitness has no selected mastery track by design.
- Introductory Skills rotate among Crow, Handstand, L-sit, Pistol squat, and
  Handstand push-up preparation according to eligibility and history.
- Equipment and recovery restrictions may reduce the available family count.

Recommended future catalogue work:

- Add introductory balance and locomotion identities that are not tied to one
  mastery progression.
- Add more low-load skill-preparation choices for recovery-constrained users.

## Equipment-specific limits

- No pull-up bar: exact Pull composition can be impossible without unsafe or
  unrelated fallback.
- No dip bars: advanced dip and Muscle-up support work remains correctly
  unavailable.
- No verified elevated surface: some Handstand push-up and single-leg
  progressions remain unavailable.
- No resistance bands: band-assisted Muscle-up transition work remains
  unavailable.

These constraints must continue to produce diagnostics rather than category
leakage, unsafe unlocks, or invented duplicate identities.
